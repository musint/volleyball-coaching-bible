# Standing Topspin Progression

## Setup

Servers spread along one 10-foot line, one ball each, cart at the sideline. Cones or a rope mark the deep third of the far court (Zones 1 and 5). Post the three stages plus the gate on a whiteboard. Every server starts at stage 1 on the first day of the block regardless of arm strength. This is the 16s topspin entry point and the whole roster runs it.

## Execution

1. **Wrist-snap at the 10-foot line.** Hold the ball in the non-hitting hand and strike it out of the hand with no toss, heel of the hand driving through the low back of the ball, fingers rolling up and over, hand finishing pointed down. The ball rotates visibly forward and drops after clearing the net. Twenty to thirty reps.
2. **Standing topspin from the end line.** Toss higher than a float toss and slightly behind it, since the contact point is higher and the arm path is a spike path. Step with the opposite foot, drive through with the hips, snap over the top. Ten reps.
3. **Depth first.** Serve the deep third only. A server owns this stage at 7 of 10 landing deep across two consecutive sets. Corners, short balls, and seams come after depth, never before.

## Coaching points

- Firm wrist is the float; wrist extension snapping through is the topspin. Never coach both in one rep.
- The snap multiplies body force; it does not replace it. No step-through, no serve.
- Contact too far forward is the most common fault. Toss back, lead with the elbow, contact above the head.
- Run the staged walk-back warm-up before any volume: start short, move back, add speed.

## Variations

- **Toss-only sets.** Ten consecutive tosses to a consistent release point before live reps resume.
- **Zone add-on.** Once depth is owned, pair with [[serve-targets]] and let the coach call the zone.
- **Video capture.** Side-view phone on a tripod; check elbow height and contact point between sets.

## Adaptations by level

- **14U and 15s:** not run. The program builds float-dominant through 15s; topspin enters at 16s.
- **HS (16s):** the full three stages, roster-wide, as the serving block's skill phase.
- **HS (17s-18s):** retention reps for servers whose topspin has gone flat, and the entry rung for late arrivals.
- **College:** diagnostic use when a jump-topspin server's contact degrades; drop back to stage 1.

**Gate.** Stage 4 of [[jump-serve-progression]] (jump-topspin, full approach) opens only after a server owns stage 3 here. Layering a spike approach onto an unowned contact pattern buys velocity and loses the serve.
