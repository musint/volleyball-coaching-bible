# Jump Serving: Three Variations

## Summary

A GMS athlete-modeling post cataloguing the top three servers after week one of
the Women's World Championships. All three average close to two aces per match,
and each generates that pressure with a different serve.

The observation is the whole post: the number one ranked server uses a jump spin,
the number two uses a two-handed-toss hybrid, and the number three uses a
one-handed jump float. Three distinct techniques producing statistically similar
results at the top of the same tournament.

For age-ladder and serve-curriculum purposes it is a useful counterweight to the
assumption that jump topspin is the terminal form of serving development. At the
highest level of the women's game, the float and hybrid families remain
competitive with spin on ace production.
