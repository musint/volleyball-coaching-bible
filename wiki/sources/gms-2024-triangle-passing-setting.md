# Triangle Passing and Setting

## Summary

A minimal GMS drill-library card for a three-player triangle ball-control drill,
run in two variations, one forearm and one overhead. It is the drill referenced
as "triangle pepper" in the GMS preseason passing plan
([[gms-2025-preseason-week-1-1-passing]]).

The entry is deliberately spare: groups of three, one ball, form a triangle, pass
or set around it with proper technique. There is no scoring, no time
prescription, and no progression. Its role in the GMS architecture is warm-up
ball control, and the triangle shape matters because it forces every contact to
change direction, which a two-person pepper line does not.

## Key claims / ideas

- GMS: form groups of three players, each group with a ball, arranged in a
  triangle.
- GMS: variation 1 is passing around the triangle with proper forearm passing
  technique.
- GMS: variation 2 is setting around the triangle with proper setting technique.
- GMS: the drill appears in the GMS preseason week-one passing plan as a warm-up
  element under the name triangle pepper.

## Topics covered

- Three-player warm-up ball control.
- Forearm passing and overhead setting in the same shape.
- Directional change on every contact.
