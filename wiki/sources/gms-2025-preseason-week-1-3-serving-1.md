# Preseason Week 1.3: Serving (1)

## Summary

The first serving session in GMS's published preseason practice-plan series, and
the companion to [[gms-2025-preseason-week-1-1-passing]]. Same architecture:
warm-up sequence, keys, activities, objectives.

The three serving keys are the substance here. "Bow and arrow: 45 degrees, elbow
and shoulder back" fixes the loaded position, "place, step, hit" fixes the
sequencing of toss and footwork, and "step and swing to target" ties direction to
the whole body rather than to the hand. The second key is given twice in the
original, once as three beats and once as four ("step, place, step, hit"), which
reads as the two-step and three-step versions of the same rhythm.

Worth noting for practice-design purposes: the serving session still runs
Butterfly The Passers as its first activity, so serving and passing are trained
against each other rather than in isolation, and the session finishes with the
same Opposite Volleyball game-like block the passing session uses.

## Key claims / ideas

- GMS: the week-one serving warm-up sequence is two-way partner passing (straight
  and simple), pass / set to self / partner pepper, dig to self / set / attack,
  and serve and chase.
- GMS: the three week-one serving keys are bow and arrow (45 degrees, elbow and
  shoulder back); "place, step, hit" given also as "step, place, step, hit"; and
  step and swing to target.
- GMS: the practice activities are Butterfly The Passers (three variations),
  Monarch Of The Court 3 vs 3 (standard), and Opposite Volleyball.
- GMS: the session objectives are to define the demands and mechanics of serving,
  to reinforce the serving keys, to use Butterfly and Monarch for high-quality
  serve and pass reps, and to use Opposite Volleyball for game-like reps.
- GMS: serving is trained against live passing from the first session. The
  serving practice plan's primary activity is a passing drill, and the serve and
  the pass are treated as two sides of one exchange.

## Topics covered

- Preseason session design for a serving-focused practice.
- GMS week-one serving cue set.
- Serve and chase as a warm-up element.
- Training the serve against live serve receive rather than in isolation.
- Monarch Of The Court as a small-sided competitive format.
