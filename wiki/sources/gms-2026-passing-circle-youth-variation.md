# Passing Circle: Youth Variation

## Summary

The passing companion to [[gms-2026-setting-circle-youth-variation]], and the
thinnest entry in this batch. Three sentences: the coach initiates from the
opposite side of the net, passers start in left back, middle back, or right back,
and athletes rotate pass to target to shag to line.

Two details still earn it a source page. The initiation comes from across the
net rather than from beside the passers, so the ball crosses the net the way a
serve does and the young passer gets a realistic flight path from the first rep.
And the starting positions are the three back-row receiving spots rather than an
arbitrary line, so the drill rehearses court position alongside contact.

Everything else about the drill (rep counts, scoring, progression) is left to the
coach.

## Key claims / ideas

- GMS: the coach initiates from the other side of the net from the passers, so
  the ball crosses the net before it is played.
- GMS: passers start in left back, middle back, or right back, which are the
  three back-row serve-receive positions.
- GMS: the role rotation is pass, then become target, then shag, then return to
  the passing line.
- GMS: it is filed as the youth variation of the passing circle, paired with the
  setting circle youth variation.

## Topics covered

- Youth passing warm-up organization.
- Across-the-net initiation for realistic ball flight.
- Back-row receiving positions as drill starting points.
- Follow-your-ball role rotation.
