# Choosing the Best Volleyball Offense: 5-1 vs 6-2 Formation Dilemma

## Summary

A second-year high school coach's post describing the roster constraint that
actually drives most system decisions at that level: the only experienced setter
is also the best libero, and she cannot be both.

The three options laid out are a 5-1 with the experienced setter (spending the
libero to get better sets), a 6-2 pairing her with one developing setter, or a
6-2 built from two developing setters so she stays at libero. The post is worth
recording because the coach reasons the tradeoff out loud rather than just asking
for an answer, and the reasoning is a clean statement of the sideout-primacy
argument: a setter who can convert an off pass into a hittable ball outweighs
digs that never get converted into points.

It is the roster-constrained counterpart to
[[gms-2025-5-1-or-6-2-how-do-you-decide]], where the decision is made from
setter-versus-setter attacking data. Here there is no data yet and no second
qualified setter, and the question is whether to spend a season developing one.

No staff answer is captured in this snapshot.

## Key claims / ideas

- GMS (field case): the binding constraint is that the only setter with
  experience is also, by a wide margin, the team's best libero, which makes the
  offense decision a defense decision at the same time.
- GMS (field case): the three options considered are a 5-1 with the experienced
  setter, a 6-2 pairing her with one developing setter, and a 6-2 built from two
  developing setters that keeps her at libero.
- GMS (field case): the coach's own reasoning is sideout-primacy. "We win and
  lose at the pins," so a setter who can reach a poorly passed ball and put up a
  hittable set outweighs a libero who digs well if the setter cannot convert.
- GMS (field case): the competing framing raised is developmental. A 6-2 spreads
  reps across two younger setters and builds depth, and the alternative is letting
  them develop on JV instead of on varsity.
- GMS (field case): last season the same coach chose a 5-1 based on chemistry
  between a senior setter and the hitters rather than on statistics, which is the
  impression-based decision that
  [[gms-2025-5-1-or-6-2-how-do-you-decide]] argues against.
