# 14s — Drill Pick List

Parent context: [[14s]] (age-guide), [[age-lens-14u]] (the club lens this list operationalizes).

## Purpose

This is the canonical 18-drill backbone for a 14s practice. The age band sits at the late-fundamentals → specialization-onset hinge: passing platforms have stabilized, hand-hinge setting is in place, the three-step approach is reliable, and **jump-float is the serve in rotation with standing-float retained as the fallback** for athletes whose toss control on the move is still maturing. The drill list reflects that arc: heavy ball-control, repeated reading reps embedded in everything, Munciana movement-and-pursuit drills as the pace backbone, a blocking-footwork block that matches what the tryout rubric actually scores, and one transition / out-of-system primer to bridge into 15s.

The selections honor the drill-mix ratio defended in [[age-lens-14u]] — roughly 30–40% ball-control, 20–30% small-group competitive, 20–30% game-like, 10% warm-up — and Swan's CAP rule that if it isn't game-like, it doesn't earn court time. Most of the list is tagged `levels: [14u, ...]` on the drill pages; the two exceptions are flagged inline below with the 14U adaptation to run instead. Everything here sits inside the cognitive load and physical envelope appropriate for adolescent female athletes per the injury and motor-learning evidence cited on the parent age-lens.

## Must-do drill list

**Warm-up (2 drills).**

- [[dynamic-warmup-volleyball]] — ball-in-hand dynamic warmup; opens every practice. Movement preparation, joint mobility, and ball-touches in one block.
- [[jump-warmup]] — light approach-and-jump primer; CSCS-compatible volume; preps neuromuscular landing patterns before the first hitting block.

**Passing (4 drills).**

- [[butterfly-passing]] — the canonical 14s passing anchor; forces feet-beat-hands and target-grading aloud.
- [[two-line-passing]] — partner-grade reps with high feedback density; pairs cleanly after butterfly.
- [[munciana-three-man-tilt]] — Munciana movement-based passing; passers tilt the platform while the ball is in flight, embedding the centering-not-reaching cue Kordes emphasizes.
- [[munciana-rush-and-push]] — two-balls-one-passer pursuit drill; trains the second-ball read and the recovery footwork that wins long rallies.

**Setting (2 drills).**

- [[target-setting]] — hand-hinge fundamentals to a static target; the entry rep that locks side-of-ball contact in.
- [[munciana-five-and-five-setting]] — set-and-cover sequence introducing out-of-system reads; the late-14s bridge to live setter-hitter timing without committing to a 5-1.

**Hitting (1 drill).**

- [[approach-and-swing]] — three-step approach with Schmidt's "start slow, accelerate the last two" tempo cue; tossed entry, then setter-fed; isolates the swing without imposing setter-timing load.

**Composite skill (1 drill).**

- [[pass-set-hit]] — the universal 14s rep: pass to target, target sets, attacker swings. Trains the three-contact rhythm that 6v6 success rests on.

**Blocking (2 drills).**

- [[block-footwork-ladder]] runs Rosen's 10-foot-line footwork progression dry. For pin blockers this is the entire year's blocking install: the jump-add is held to late 15s, so a pin blocker who starts jumping her swing-block at 14s gets the jump taken back out. Top-club middles are the exception and complete the footwork-plus-jump progression by mid-season.
- [[block-touch-drill]] covers hand position and net penetration. Run its **14U adaptation**: coach hits straight at a single blocker and the win is hands above the tape on every rep, rather than the HS touch-rate format. The drill page is tagged `levels: [hs, college]` for the full version; the adaptation is what belongs at 14s.

**Serving (3 drills).**

- [[serve-targets]] — accuracy-first float serving to numbered zones; reps Dunning's toss-first emphasis without pressure-load. Run it with jump-float servers as well as standing-float ones, because the target discipline is the point, not the serve type.
- [[jump-serve-progression]] is the jump-float install vehicle, and the reason jump-float is in rotation at 14s rather than aspirational. **Stages 1-3 only at 14s and 15s** (standing float, one-step float, two-step jump-float). **Stage 4 is jump-topspin and is 16+ in this program; do not run it here**, however ready a server looks. Use the drill's own gates rather than coach judgement: 10 consecutive tosses through a consistent release point before a stage is attempted, and 7 of 10 to a called zone across two consecutive sets before the next stage opens.
- [[munciana-no-gut-serving]] — light-pressure serving with consequence; introduces a pressure-acclimatization element appropriate at 14s without the all-or-nothing stakes of a wash drill.

**Defense (1 drill).**

- [[munciana-go-stay]] — read-based defense: the defender either pursues or holds based on a triggered cue; the cleanest 14s introduction to Look/See/Decide diagnosis on real attacks.

**Game-like / cooperative (1 drill).**

- [[cooperative-25-goal]] — Kessel-style cooperative target; teams chase a shared count of clean three-contact sequences. The grills-not-drills test in one rep.

**Transition primer (1 drill).**

- [[munciana-mia-drill]] — defender sets out-of-system to an attacker; the late-14s gateway into transition reads. Run as a concept block, not a stress-tested install.

## When-in-season-to-use mapping

The pick-list is tagged `season-context: composite` because at 14s the seasonal arc is itself a development arc — a "preseason-only" backbone would shortchange a club calendar that runs November through May. Use the list as follows:

- **Preseason (Nov–early Dec).** Lean into the warm-up + ball-control block (dynamic-warmup-volleyball, jump-warmup, butterfly-passing, two-line-passing, target-setting, approach-and-swing). Re-establish movement quality and reset platform/hand-hinge fundamentals. Start block-footwork-ladder here — dry footwork costs nothing and the pattern needs months, not weeks. Run jump-serve-progression from week one so the toss gates are cleared before the serve matters. Run cooperative-25-goal as the closing rep — chemistry-first, per Rose's club-rules framing.
- **Mid-season (Dec–Feb, local-qualifier window).** Add the Munciana movement drills (munciana-three-man-tilt, munciana-rush-and-push, munciana-five-and-five-setting, munciana-go-stay). These raise the read-and-pursue density without imposing wash-drill anxiety on under-construction motor programs. Add block-touch-drill at its 14U adaptation once the ladder footwork holds up at speed; this is also the window where top-club middles add the jump to their swing-block.
- **Pre-tournament (regionals, March–April).** Add munciana-no-gut-serving for low-stakes pressure-acclimatization, and munciana-mia-drill to install the out-of-system look. Keep cooperative-25-goal as the closer; a high-confidence final block matters more than a final stress test at 14s.
- **Taper / match-day.** Pull the Munciana pursuit and out-of-system drills out; keep the warm-up block, butterfly-passing, target-setting, approach-and-swing, serve-targets, and cooperative-25-goal. Reps that build confidence; nothing that introduces new failure modes the day before competition.
- **Tryout / team-formation week.** Run the warm-up block, butterfly-passing (graded), target-setting, approach-and-swing, block-footwork-ladder, and serve-targets — the rubric-friendly, scorable subset. Every one of these maps to a row on [[tryout-rubric-14u]], which is the point: train what you score.

## Adaptations

- **Newer 14s teams (first-year-of-6s, late tryouts).** Cap pursuit drills (rush-and-push, go-stay) at half-volume; substitute extra reps of two-line-passing and target-setting. Skip munciana-mia-drill until late-season.
- **Veteran 14s teams (second-year, nationally competitive).** Increase munciana-five-and-five-setting volume; add a pressure-tier to munciana-no-gut-serving (consequence reps); run pass-set-hit with a live setter rather than a target.
- **Mixed-experience rosters.** Run butterfly-passing and target-setting as the levelers; veterans grade newer passers aloud; cooperative-25-goal mixes the lines so the chemistry rep is real.
- **Short-session days (60-min practice).** Keep warm-up + butterfly-passing + target-setting + approach-and-swing + cooperative-25-goal. Five drills; complete arc; no skill block neglected.
- **Athlete-load caution (post-tournament, growth-spurt visible).** Pull jump-warmup volume; pull munciana-rush-and-push (the highest-pursuit drill) entirely. The injury-prevention frame on the parent age-lens applies at the daily-plan level, not just the macrocycle.
