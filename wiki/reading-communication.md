# Reading and communication

## Overview

Reading is the perceptual skill of knowing where the ball is going before it gets there. Communication is the audible layer that converts one player's read into six players' action. They share a hub because they develop on the same clock: a player who cannot see the play has nothing worth saying, and a team whose reads stay silent defends as six individuals standing near each other.

Both are trainable and both have age-specific entry points, which is why they sit on the spine instead of under temperament. Treating reading as innate ("she just sees the game") and talking as a personality question ("she is quiet") leaves both untaught.

The assessment rule here follows from one sourced distinction: understanding is not intention. A player can explain the ball-setter-ball-hitter sequence perfectly, answer every "what did you see?" correctly, and still move on the hitter's plant every rally. Trinsey keeps the two tests apart: whether an athlete understood the instruction is a different diagnostic from what she was trying to do, and an athlete who answers "what were you trying to do there?" by describing her footwork has told you her intention drifted [trinsey-2026-good-intentions]. Every promotion criterion below is therefore an eye position or an early movement visible in a rep block. Recitation builds a read; it does not certify one.

Underneath sits perception-action coupling: change what an athlete looks at and you change how she moves, which is why Trinsey starts new movement teaching with what the player will see [trinsey-2026-do-habits-exist-part-1]. The same logic keeps reading work inside live tasks against real servers and hitters.

## Major subtopics

- **[[defense-reading]]**: the back-row read. Kiraly's ball-setter-ball-hitter eye sequence, Josephson's look / see / decide diagnostic, and the arm-shape cues (back, bent, straight) worth an extra third of a second. The four-way school disagreement is worked out in full there.
- **[[blocking-read]]**: the same machinery at the net, plus the commit-versus-read decision and the setter keys (body angle off the net, hand position, release tempo) driving it. Feeds [[read-blocking]] and [[commit-blocking]].
- **[[passing-serve-receive]]**: reading the server. Toss height, arm speed, shoulder line, and the seam calls that make a receive formation a unit.

Communication has no standalone technique page by design. Calling standards live inside each cue dictionary ([[passing-cues]], [[defense-cues]], [[blocking-cues]], [[transition-cues]]), because a call is a cue spoken by a player and lands best in the skill context that produced it. [[cues]] is the hub over all seven.

## Teaching by age band

### 10-12: Foundation

**Introduce** the two earliest reads: watch the server before contact (toss height, arm speed), and read ball flight once it crosses. Trinsey's beginner ladder gives the sequencing. Start with the single question "is it coming over?" using deliberately obvious visual cues from the coach, and layer the Off / On / Over distinction only once that read is reliable [trinsey-2026-bsbh-variations]. **Develop** the two Foundation calls, "mine" and "out," inside [[serve-receive-4-player]]. **Refine** nothing yet; the eye-sequence layer is deferred.

*In-band year gates.* At **11s**, the standard is calling "free" or "hit" before the ball crosses the net on 6 of 10 coach-fed balls, and calling "hands" or "platform" on 8 of 10 balls fed above and below the shoulders [[11s]]. At **12s**, mine / yours / out is expected inside the four-player formation and look / see / decide enters as drill language for top-club rosters; B-S-B-H as a separate diagnostic stays 13s and up [[12s]].

*Observable promotion criterion.* The feet or shoulders change before the ball crosses the plane on most fed balls, and the "mine" call lands early enough for a teammate to hear it and release. See [[10s]], [[tryout-rubric-12s]], [[12s-drills]], [[passing-cues]].

### 13-14: Rebuild

**Introduce** attacker-approach reading (shot versus swing, off the hips, shoulder line, and hand) and, at **14s**, the structured eyework sequence: ball to setter, back to ball, then hitter, taught verbally and coached inside drills [aoc-2024-kiraly-reads-every-skill]. **Develop** look / see / decide as a coach-prompted diagnostic so you can tell which of the three beats failed [aoc-2025-training-reading-look-see-decide]. **Develop** the calling layer past ball ownership into formation work: seam calls, switch calls, out-of-system detect / call / bail-to-pin. **Refine** the Foundation calls until they stop needing prompting.

*In-band year gates.* At **13s**, reading is embedded in every ball-control drill rather than run as its own drill; even "where is the setter when the ball arrives?" counts [[13s]]. At **14s**, B-S-B-H is introduced, unprompted verbalization is explicitly a 15s standard, and the real-time calling expectation is balls, seams, and switches during play [[14s]].

*Observable promotion criterion.* Against a live hitter, the defender's first movement changes her depth or lane **before** the attacker's contact on most swings, which is the visible form of shot-versus-swing recognition. See [[defense-reading]], [[tryout-rubric-14u]], [[14s-drills]], [[transition-out-of-system]], [[defense-cues]].

### 15-16: Refinement

**Introduce** the full blocking read sequence: eyes off the pass early, on the setter for a long time, on the hitter by the time the blocker jumps, sooner against fast offenses [gms-2025-learn-the-bunch-read-blocking-system]. Trinsey coaches the first handoff as "Ball-SetterSetterSetter," because blockers who follow the pass into the hands react late to the set [trinsey-2026-bsbh-variations]. **Introduce** bunch-read awareness at 16s, where the pin blocker's read becomes "is this my set?" before she releases. **Develop** full-court anticipation: setter eyes, setter position off the net, block shape, and set location read together. **Refine** the calling standard to call-early-call-loud, with seam conventions fixed in advance [aoc-2024-sr-team-communication].

*In-band year gates.* At **15s**, B-S-B-H and look / see / decide become diagnostic tools the athlete is expected to verbalize on request, the tryout standard for that year [[15s]]. At **16s**, the passer reads the server's toss-and-shoulder line before contact, the defender reads setter eyes and calls coverage adjustments, and call-early-call-loud is non-negotiable [[16s]].

*Observable promotion criterion.* On video, the blocker's eyes are on the setter before the pass reaches the setter's hands, and her block move starts on the ball leaving the hands rather than on the hitter's plant. See [[blocking-read]], [[read-blocking]], [[blocking-cues]], [[tryout-rubric-15s]], [[16s-drills]].

### 17-18: Performance

**Introduce** opponent-tendency integration: scouting output becomes a pre-serve read rather than a pre-match briefing. McCutcheon frames it as reducing uncertainty by recognizing recurring competitive patterns, the single-rally read scaled to a match [mccutcheon-2022-championship-behaviors]. **Develop** tactical talk: audibles keyed to pass quality, tempo called live, coverage shapes named aloud. **Refine** reading under fatigue and pressure. Degradation shows up first in serve receive, so pressure rehearsal there is non-optional [aoc-2024-kiraly-reads-every-skill].

*In-band year gates.* At **17s**, pre-serve communication on seam responsibilities is non-negotiable, and the setter and libero can call the four-passer adjustment against a strong jump-topspin server with no coach prompt [aoc-2024-sr-formations] [[17s]]. At **18s**, pre-serve seam-ownership communication is audible and instant, and the setter calls tempo in real time instead of running the bench call [[18s]].

*Observable promotion criterion.* The libero and setter execute that formation adjustment inside a live set with no bench input, and the blocker's dedicate step toward a scouted attacker happens while the pass is still in the air [gms-2025-learn-the-bunch-read-blocking-system]. See [[match-prep]], [[match-prep-opponent-tendency-form]], [[18s-drills]], [[libero]], [[setter]].

## Schools of thought

- **[[art-of-coaching-volleyball]]**: reading is a named, standalone perceptual skill with explicit cues, a canonical eye sequence, and coach questioning as the teaching method [aoc-2024-kiraly-reads-every-skill] [aoc-2025-training-reading-look-see-decide]. Kiraly extends the questioning stance to the learning culture, dosing feedback to each athlete's bandwidth [aoc-2024-kiraly-asking-questions].
- **[[gold-medal-squared]]**: eyework is explicit and named, trained blocked-to-variable, because blockers stressed about the read revert to old mechanics [gms-2025-learn-the-bunch-read-blocking-system]. GMS declines to prescribe pre-contact movement in serve receive, framing the goal as functional variability [gms-2025-should-players-move-before-contact].
- **[[ecological-dynamics]]**: reading is direct perception of affordances rather than cue decoding, so practice must preserve perception-action coupling [pinder-2011-representative-learning-design]. Expert defenders attend to the relational spaces between setter, hitter, and blocker [afonso-2012-volleyball-perceptual-cognitive].
- **[[japanese-training]]**: reading arrives through volume and a dig-everything expectation rather than as a separately named skill.

Two cells stay open. **Program default pending** on the second handoff: Trinsey reports the ball-to-hitter transition as genuinely contested, some coaches holding blockers on the ball longer to line up with it and others moving to the hitter earlier, and leaves it unresolved [trinsey-2026-bsbh-variations]. **Program default pending** on the promotion gate: the age-guide standard at [[15s]] is unprompted verbalization of B-S-B-H, while this page grades movement only. They coexist as a coach diagnostic and a promotion gate, and the program has yet to pick one instrument.

One practical note survives all four schools: scripted reps are easier rather than better. They remove a decision so attention can go elsewhere, and live reps are what they feed [trinsey-2026-bsbh-variations].
