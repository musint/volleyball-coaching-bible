# Tryout Rubric: 14U (14s)

## Purpose

This rubric scores tryout candidates against the [[14s]] `## Promotion criteria` contract on a 1-5 scale. It is the **operational counterpart** to that age-guide section: every row maps directly to a promotion criterion, and column 5 in every row describes a 15s-ready athlete. Use it for your nationally-ranked club 14U tryout, and reuse it mid-season as a re-evaluation form to track movement toward 15s readiness. The voice and operational depth follow [[mike-lingenfelter]] and [[munciana-volleyball-club]]: short windows, observable behaviors, no projection beyond what the candidate demonstrates in the gym today. Cross-reference [[age-lens-14u]] for the developmental ceilings the rubric should not over-reach.

**Column 5 of every row is the next age's column-3 standard; it flags readiness, it is not install content for this age.** Each column-5 cell below is written against the [[tryout-rubric-15s]] column-3 anchors so the two rubrics dovetail at the seam. Scoring a 5 tells you where to place an athlete; it does not license teaching 15s content to the 14s group.

Pass grades use the 0-3 scale whose anchors are defined once at [[passing-serve-receive]]. "In-system" on this page means a pass graded **2 or better**: it lands inside the setter zone, a six-foot-wide box centred on the setter's base at the net, and it peaks at seven feet or higher so she can play it standing up.

## Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | 1: Does not demonstrate | 2: Occasional | 3: Consistent at 14s (on-target) | 4: Above age-appropriate | 5: 15s-ready (= [[tryout-rubric-15s]] column 3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Passing** | Reach-and-pop platform; chest-trap on jam balls; no pass-and-cover habit | Forearm pass occasionally lands in target zone (<40%); hops back inconsistently; quiet platform under tossed balls only | ≥60% in-system (grade 2+) off moderate-pace standing-float; quiet platform with hop-back on jam; pass-and-cover sprint visible after contact; deliberate forearm-vs-overhead read | ≥75% in-system off live-paced float; consistent under jump-float; recovers cleanly off seam balls; coverage-spot habit reflexive | Platform stable across serve types; pass-grade 2.0+ against age-peer servers; overhead pass used on shoulder-height balls; B-S-B-H read articulable when prompted |
| **Setting** (setter candidates only; non-candidates score N/A, see Scoring) | Chest-trap on overhead set; setter pushes through with wrists rather than hinging; no Tempo vocabulary | Hand-hinge inconsistent; outside set delivers a hittable ball ~half the time; backset telegraphs (turns shoulders early) | Hand-hinge with side-of-ball contact; outside set hittable from in-system pass; backset competent without telegraphing; demonstrates Tempo 3 footwork on tossed balls | Tempo 2 sets executable on in-system passes; jump-set off tight passes; varies tempo to opposing block; Tempo 1 footwork demo'd | Hand-hinge with side-of-ball contact locked in; 5-1 run across three rotations; jump-set on at-net tempo balls; backset without shoulder dip |
| **Hitting** | Two-step or flat-tempo approach; flat sidearm swing; no shot inventory beyond "hit hard" | Three-step approach inconsistent; arm swing with low elbow; one shot in inventory | Three-step approach with visible "start slow, accelerate the last two" tempo; elbow-lead, high-hand contact; ≥2 of {big cross, line, tip} usable; female-specific approach-velocity coachable from cue | Approach reliable from out-of-system tosses too; full {big cross, line, tip} inventory situational; angle-vs-line decision pre-contact; four-step directional first step layered on late-season; back-row attack repped in controlled drills | 4-step approach with start-slow / accelerate-the-last-two; three reliable shots (cross and line plus one of angle/tip/roll); high-hand contact on most reps; pipe available as a mid-set option |
| **Blocking** | No closing position at the pin; flat-foot block jumps; default landing is run-back (knee-valgus risk) | Footwork inconsistent; hand penetration shallow; lands stick *or* run-back, no default | Static-and-shuffle reliable for pin blockers; outside-foot-forward closing stance; hand-press with net penetration; default landing is stick or step-back, **never run-back**; **full swing-block execution (footwork + jump) emerging for middles at top-club 14s, pins footwork-only all year** | Full swing-block (footwork + jump) reliable for middles; pin blockers move cleanly through the Rosen 10-foot-line footwork-only progression; closes seam reliably; reads setter eyes pre-jump | Stick or step-back landing is the unprompted default; bunch-read applied against opponents whose middle threat is real; full swing-block is the middles' default at-pin scheme, with the pin-blocker jump-add arriving by end of the 15s year |
| **Serving** | Toss inconsistent; clears net <60% on standing-float; no target intent, only "deep" | Standing-float reliable to half-court; jump-float toss attempted on the move but inconsistent; aim is general zone, not specific spot | **Jump-float in rotation (toss consistency is the gate, not contact); standing-float retained as in-zone fallback (≥60% in target zone)**; aim-small target accountability, names spot before serve | Jump-float reliable in matches at moderate pace; standing-float ≥75% to call zone; runs short-deep mix on serve; clears the toss gates (10 consecutive tosses through one release point; 7 of 10 to a called zone on two consecutive sets) | Jump-float is her primary serve; standing-float retained as the in-zone backup; aim-small-miss-small target accountability on every serve. Topspin stays 16+ and is never what earns this column |
| **Defense** | Static feet on contact; reaches with platform high; no read on shot-vs-swing; no pursuit on tips | Three P's inconsistent; platform formed too early or too late; reads ball, not hitter | Three P's (posture, position, passion, glossed as get back / get low / get a touch) habitual, not coach-prompted; Look/See/Decide read on shot-vs-swing pre-contact; platform formation late, not early | Reads hitter approach-shape pre-contact; pursues tips from off-position; arrives stopped before platform forms | Hips-under-ball readable on hard-driven digs; Three P's executed under pursuit reps; can name which defensive system is on the floor and her role inside it |
| **Transition** | Run-back landing default after blocks; no recovery footwork off dig; panic-sets with hands when tight pass arrives | Block-to-approach footwork inconsistent; OOS recognition slow; setter dump or hand-set tight balls | Block-to-approach footwork (step-back default); dig-to-approach footwork live for OHs/opposites; OOS recognition triggers forearm-set or bump-to-teammate, not panic-set | Sub-2-second block-to-approach recovery; OOS-detect-call-bail-to-pin protocol clean; bail ball lands on the five-and-five target and cover hands hit the floor before the ball is caught | Block-to-approach and dig-to-approach both automatic; OOS detect-call-bail-to-pin reliable on the bail-to-pin half |
| **Mental & coachability** | Defensive reactivity to feedback; effort drops with score; phone-checks during teammate reps; no in-rally communication | Compliant but silent on cue; effort uneven; communicates retrospectively, not in real time | Self-corrects after **one** cue (not three); consistent effort independent of score; communicates seams/switches/balls in-rally; engaged during teammate reps | Coach-style self-feedback emerging; teammate-correcting in-rally; visibly resets after errors within one rep | Sustained engagement with self-talk or a pre-performance routine over a multi-month block; differentiates process from result feedback; takes a hard correction in front of teammates without defensiveness |

## Scoring

- **Per-row scoring:** every evaluator scores each row 1-5 independently. Eight rows, so the composite is the **sum of the scored rows**, maximum 40.
- **Non-setters: the N/A formula.** Setting is the only position-conditional row. A non-setter candidate is scored on the other **seven** rows, and her composite is **(sum of seven rows) × 8/7, rounded to the nearest whole number**. That puts her on the same 8-40 scale as a setter candidate without inventing a Setting score. Worked example: 7 rows totalling 24 gives 24 × 8/7 = 27.4, which rounds to **27**. Do not enter a zero for Setting, and do not silently compare a 7-row sum against an 8-row threshold.
- **Placement bands are row averages, not sums.** Divide the composite by 8 (or work directly from the seven-row average for a non-setter, since the scaling above leaves the average unchanged). Averages are used so the bands survive both the N/A adjustment and any position weighting a staff chooses to apply.
  - **Below 2.0.** Development focus. Name the two weakest rows and build the season plan around them.
  - **2.0 to 2.9.** Age-appropriate at 14s. Standard 14s placement.
  - **3.0 to 3.9.** Promotes; 15s-tracking. This is the [[14s]] promotion contract met.
  - **4.0+.** Accelerated grouping. Pull her into a 15s rep pool during specialized blocks even if she is rostered at 14s.
- **The coachability gate is non-negotiable and sits outside the bands.** A candidate scoring below 3 on Mental & coachability does not promote regardless of her average. Skill ceilings are coachable; coachability deficits compound and gate everything else.
- **If you weight rows by position** (double-weighting the two rows that define the position is reasonable), compute the **weighted average**: multiply each row by its weight, sum, and divide by the sum of the weights. Dividing by eight after doubling two rows inflates the average by roughly a quarter and moves athletes into bands they have not earned.
- **Scores of 1** in any row trigger a one-cue re-evaluation rep before final scoring; a candidate may be misreading the drill format, not the skill.

## Calibration notes

- **Run a 60-90-minute tryout** with these blocks: dynamic warmup (10) → passing block off tossed + standing-float serves (15) → setter-segregated tempo demo + non-setter hitting block (15) → blocking footwork + landing-pattern check (10) → serving target work, both standing-float and jump-float toss (10) → defense Three P's + Look/See/Decide reads (10) → 6v6 wash-drill segment for transition + mental scoring (15-20). Score after the block ends, not during reps.
- **Inter-rater agreement:** ≥2 evaluators per court; reconcile any score that differs by ≥2 between raters before finalizing. Use the column-3 anchor description as the calibration target, "what would a 14s-on-target athlete look like in this drill right now", to anchor the disagreement conversation.
- **Common biases to avoid:** (a) projection, scoring physical upside instead of demonstrated skill; (b) returner-bias, over-scoring known athletes from prior years on memory; (c) growth-spurt confound, under-scoring an awkward-phase athlete whose mechanics will re-stabilize within a season; (d) one-skill halo, strong server gets a 4 in passing because she "looks like a player." Score what the gym shows today, write your one-line rationale, and let row-totals speak.
- **Run-back landing is a hard rule, not a scoring nuance.** Any candidate landing run-back-default after blocks scores ≤2 on Blocking row regardless of footwork quality elsewhere [zahradnik-2014-block-landing-grf-valgus]. This is an injury-prevention floor, not a stylistic preference.
- **Do not reward a pin blocker for adding the jump.** The jump-add for pin blockers is held to late 15s in this program; a 14s pin blocker jumping her swing-block before her footwork is automatic is scored on the footwork, not credited for the jump [aoc-2026-swing-blocking-footwork-beginners]. Middles are the exception and the column-3 wording says so.
- **Do not reward a topspin serve.** The program serve curriculum is float-dominant through 15s. A candidate who shows topspin is scored on what her float does; topspin is neither a bonus nor a deduction, it is simply not what these columns measure.
- **Female-specific approach-velocity is coachable, not strength-bound.** Do not down-score short-stature candidates whose approach-velocity is a technique gap rather than a power gap [fuchs-2019-male-female-spike-biomechanics]: score her column-3 if technique is corrigible from cue.
- **Munciana operational depth applies directly here:** short windows, fast scoring resets, drill volume in tight geometric formats, the rubric is designed for the format Munciana runs, not for long-line waiting [munciana-2022-camp-drills]. If your tryout uses long passing lines, the rubric scores noise.
