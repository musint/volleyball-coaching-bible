# Tryout Rubric: 12s

## Purpose

This rubric translates the 27 promotion criteria from [[12s]] `## Promotion criteria` into a **9-row, 1-5 evaluation grid** usable in a 60-90-minute tryout for entering 12s players (rising 12s) or returning 12s players being evaluated for 13s readiness. Cross-reference [[age-lens-14u]] for program-level context, and [[12s-drills]] for the drill catalog the rubric assumes evaluators are pulling stations from.

**The 27 criteria are pass/fail observations that roll up into these 9 rows, not 27 independent scales.** Each criterion in [[12s]] names the row it belongs to. Read the criteria list before the tryout so every evaluator is watching for the same behaviors; score the nine rows on the clipboard. Rolling up is a judgment: a row scores 3 when its criteria are consistently present at age-appropriate level, 2 when they appear occasionally, 1 when they are not observable.

**Column 3 is the promotion signal.** It is the on-track 12s baseline: consistent demonstration at age-appropriate level.

**Column 5 of every row is the next age's column-3 standard; it flags readiness, it is not install content for this age.** Every column-5 cell below restates the corresponding column-3 cell on [[tryout-rubric-13s]]. A player scoring 5 across most rows is at the 13s on-track baseline and should be flagged for a higher-team conversation. This is what resolves the jump-float question: jump-float appears in the Serving column-5 cell as a **13s-side observation**, never as a 12s install. Topspin variants stay 16+ in this program, and jump-float toss work belongs to 13s.

## Evaluation criteria

| Skill | 1 — Not yet | 2 — Occasional | 3 — Consistent age-appropriate | 4 — Above age-appropriate | 5 — 13s-ready (= [[tryout-rubric-13s]] column 3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Passing** (forearm + overhead; criteria 1-5) | Reaches across body, no platform formation, hops into contact, no overhead pass attempt | Platform forms inconsistently; sometimes stops, often hops; overhead only on emergency | Stops & balances, thumbs together, sets the angle with shoulders and hips on a tossed ball, meeting the 7' target 7/10; overhead on free balls; calls "mine" | Late-platform formation reliable; shuffle footwork without prompt; overhead clean to target | ≥6/10 **medium-pace serves** to a 6-ft target with platform-not-arm-swing; quiet platform under pace. |
| **Setting** (criteria 6-8) | Palms-up, ball goes straight up; underhand-set fallback; eyes locked on ball | Hand-hinge attempted, side-of-ball contact rare; front-set only | Hand-hinge with side-of-ball contact; front and back set distinguishable; eyes-on-target on tossed ball | Live-set without catch scaffold; locating to OH/opp zones; tempo 3 reliably | Hand-hinge **live** (no catch-and-set scaffold at all); front-set to the outside antenna ≥6/10. |
| **Hitting** (criteria 9-11, 25) | No approach pattern; one-foot takeoff; arm-only swing; lands moving backward | Three-step inconsistent; bow-and-arrow load missing; wrist-snap habit visible | L-R-L approach with start-slow-accelerate; bow-and-arrow load; contact out front; sticks landing | Big cross-court swing with high contact; closes the heel; consistent target hitting | 3-step rhythmic; both feet under the hitter at finish; high elbow audible in the swing. |
| **Blocking** (criteria 12-13) | Doesn't reach over net; crosses feet; ball hits chest | Hand-press shape inconsistent; outside drifts middle on tempo-3 sets | Static-and-shuffle middle/pin without crossing feet; thumbs-up press; holds the program pin stance (outside foot slightly ahead, hips angled into the court) | Penetrates over net plane consistently; reads set before commit; recovers off net | Static-and-shuffle clean; hand-press established; partial penetration over the net plane. |
| **Serving** (criteria 14-16) | Underhand only or overhand fails to clear net; toss across body | Float clears net 3-5/10; toss isolated drill not yet repeatable; no target awareness | Standing float clears net 8/10, lands in zone 6/10; lift-step-swing visible; isolated toss repeatable | Zone serving 8/10; aim-small-miss-small mindset; aggressive contact out front | ≥7/10 in-zone at the 13s service line; toss shoulder-height in front of the hitting shoulder. *(At 13s, jump-float toss-only work sits above this line and topspin variants stay 16+; neither is 12s content.)* |
| **Defense** (criteria 17-19, 24) | Upright stance, weight on heels; no platform on hard-driven; no ball-call | Three P's attempted; hips inconsistent; calls "ball" sometimes | Get back / get low / get a touch on a coach down-ball; hips under hard-driven dig; calls before arrival; perimeter outside-foot-high stance held | Reads hitter shoulder pre-contact; emergency overhead pass clean; perimeter base instinctive | Three P's visible on hard-driven **and tip**; hips-under-ball platform; eyes track setter → hitter on coach prompt. |
| **Transition** (criterion 20) | Static after contact; no recovery footwork | Free-ball-to-offense in cooperative drill only; no live transition | Free-ball pass-set-hit cleanly in cooperative drill; recognizes free-ball call | Block-to-approach footwork visible; dig-to-approach attempted in live play | Identifies a free ball, moves to base, and executes a controlled pass-set-hit at moderate tempo; block-to-approach recovery footwork present. |
| **Mental / coachability** (criteria 21-23) | No self-correction; eyes wander during instruction; emotional bail after miss | Self-corrects sometimes; repeats cue inconsistently; off-rep disengaged | Self-corrects between reps; repeats cue accurately; engages teammates' reps; FeedForward-receptive | Initiates teammate cue; coach-modeled self-talk visible; competes in low-stakes pressure games | One-cue self-correct; bilateral landings knees-over-toes; emergent self-talk vocabulary. |
| **Systems** (criteria 26-27) | Loses her rotation; cannot name her position; needs the coach on every serve | Names her position with help; rotation tracking breaks under a lineup change; overlap not understood | **Plays a position in a 6-2 (or transitional 4-2 early season) without needing rotation help from the coach; calls overlap correctly when asked** ("am I behind or in front of my setter?") | Tracks all six rotations through substitutions; corrects a teammate's overlap before the whistle; explains why the switch happens | Runs the 6-2 without a whiteboard; 5-1 concepts introduced for setter candidates. |

## Scoring

Each evaluator scores all **9 rows** per athlete on the 1-5 scale. Total = 9-45.

**Promotion gate to [[13s]], stated identically here and in [[12s]] `## Promotion criteria`:**

> A 12s athlete promotes to [[13s]] on **3 or higher on at least 8 of the 9 rubric rows, AND no score of 1 in Passing, Setting, or Serving.**

In plain terms: **at most one row below standard, and never one of the three that matter most.** This is the program's long-standing "at most one row below 3," re-denominated from 7-of-8 to 8-of-9 when the Systems row was added. The bar did not move.

Score 4-5 on 4+ rows indicates ahead-of-age-band development. Flag for a higher-team conversation. A score of 1 in Passing, Setting, or Serving blocks promotion regardless of the other rows.

The 27-criterion checklist in [[12s]] `## Promotion criteria` is the reading list that tells you what each cell observes; **the 9-row table is the only thing scored.** Do not score the 27 criteria individually and do not average them; they are pass/fail observations that roll up.

## Calibration notes

**Session structure (60-90 min).** Run stations: 15 min passing (tossed + easy-served), 10 min setting (target station), 10 min hitting (approach + tossed-set), 8 min serving (zone targets), 10 min defense (coach down-ball + hard-driven), 15 min 6v6 cooperative-then-low-stakes-competitive observing **transition, blocking, mental, and Systems**. The Systems row is scored in the 6v6 block only, because rotation tracking and overlap are not observable in a station.

**Train what you score.** The Blocking row is only fair if the team has rehearsed blocking footwork. [[12s-drills]] carries [[block-footwork-ladder]] (shuffle and crossover patterns at this age, no swing-run) for exactly this reason. Scoring a skill the program never gave reps at measures an athlete's background, not your coaching.

**Inter-rater agreement.** Use 2-3 evaluators per athlete; the pre-tryout meeting walks all **9 rows** and anchors examples for column 3 and column 5. Score independently, reconcile differences >1 column post-session. Sample 3 athletes mid-session to check evaluator drift.

**Checkpoint re-scoring.** These nine rows are re-scored at week 6 and week 12 per [[12s]] `## Season operations`. Use this document unchanged at those checkpoints.

**Common biases to avoid.** Height-halo (tall ≠ skilled at 12s — Munciana flags this). Friend-of-coach bias. Recency bias (last station scored harshly). Confusing volume of effort with skill quality — a quiet, balanced platform outscores enthusiastic flailing every time.

**Recency lean.** Per the program preference for modern training, score against AOC/Munciana cue language and hand-hinge setting standards rather than legacy underhand-set or wrist-snap-spike framings.
