# Tryout Rubric: 11s

## Purpose

This rubric evaluates 11U athletes against the [[11s]] promotion criteria — specifically, what an 11U player must demonstrate to graduate into [[12s]]. Use it for end-of-season evaluations, mid-year level checks, and tryout placement at the 11U/12U boundary.

**Column 3 is the promotion signal.** Column 3 ("consistent at age-appropriate level") is the on-track 11U state, and an athlete sitting at 3s across the board is exactly what an 11s graduate is supposed to look like. She promotes.

**Column 5 of every row is the next age's column-3 standard; it flags readiness, it is not install content for this age.** Every column-5 cell below restates the corresponding column-3 cell on [[tryout-rubric-12s]]. Column 5 **flags a higher-team or accelerated-grouping conversation**: it is a scouting note, not a bar an 11s athlete is expected to clear, and nothing in it belongs in an 11s practice plan.

All counts and distances in the cells below are copied verbatim from [[11s]] `## Skill milestones` and `## Promotion criteria`. Where the two documents disagree, the age-guide wins and this one gets fixed.

The rubric inherits the wiki's recency-leaning operating model from [[age-lens-14u]]: modern AOC and Munciana coaching language is the assumed cue vocabulary, simple [[offense-4-2]] is the assumed entry-level offense, and "introduction phase transitioning toward fundamentals" is the assumed developmental window.

**Serving distances are not restated here.** The 11s serving-line progression is published once, in [[11s]] `## Skill milestones` → Serving, and referenced from [[11s-drills]] and from this rubric. Score every athlete from **her current line in that progression**, and write down which line that is. An athlete serving cleanly from the attack line and an athlete serving cleanly from the end line both score 3.

## Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | 1 — does not demonstrate | 2 — occasional | 3 — consistent at age-appropriate level | 4 — above age-appropriate | 5 — 12s-ready (= [[tryout-rubric-12s]] column 3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Passing** | No platform shape; arms apart, reaches without moving feet. | Platform shape on centered balls only; reaches on off-center balls. | Recognizable platform on 8/10 contacts; shuffles toward the ball on 6/10 balls fed 3-5 ft to either side; thumbs together; passes into the setter zone on 6/10. | Platform holds on off-center balls; shuffle-not-reach without prompt; aims with the shoulders rather than tilting an arm. | Stops and balances, thumbs together, sets the angle with shoulders and hips on a tossed ball; overhead pass on free balls; calls "mine." |
| **Setting** | No hand triangle; thumbs collide; palms catch. | Triangle attempted but inconsistent; double-contact violations frequent. | Catch-and-set with hand triangle and no thumb collision on 8/10; calls "hands" or "platform" correctly on 8/10 balls fed above and below the shoulders. | Hand-hinge mechanism present in catch-and-set; setter-track candidates live-set tossed balls inconsistently. | Hand-hinge with side-of-ball contact; front-set and back-set distinguishable; eyes on target rather than on the ball at contact on a tossed ball. |
| **Hitting** | No swing shape; pushes the ball; no approach. | Down-ball arm swing attempted; no approach footwork. | Down-ball swing from puppet position (elbow up, contact in front) on 7/10; 2-step Flamingo approach with isolated jump on 5/10; at least 3 attempts at a 3-step. | 2-step approach onto an accurate toss with isolated jump consistently; contact in front every rep; 3-step recognizable. | L-R-L three-step approach with start-slow-accelerate tempo; bow-and-arrow load; contact out front; sticks the landing. |
| **Blocking** | Hands stay below the tape at the top of the jump; backs away from the net; net contact. | Hands reach the tape occasionally; jump uncommitted. | **Hands at or above the tape at the top of a controlled stationary jump** on 6/10, thumbs up, fingers spread, no net contact; chooses block-or-back-up correctly on 6/10 coach-called situations. | Balanced pre-jump posture every rep; no retreat from the net; block-or-back-up decision automatic. | Static-and-shuffle footwork in the middle and at the pin without crossing the feet; thumbs-up hand press; holds the program pin stance. |
| **Serving** | Underhand does not clear the net reliably; no overhand attempt. | Underhand reliable from the end line; overhand attempted, contact rarely clean. | Underhand 7/10 in-court from the end line; **overhand successful contact on 3/10** from her current line in the published progression; toss 8/10 into a 2-ft box with the swing withheld. | Overhand successful contact well above 3/10; lands in the correct half of the receiving court on 4/10 in-court serves; has earned a line further back without losing contact quality. | Standing float clears the net 8/10 and lands in a called zone 6/10; lift-step-swing visible; isolated toss drill repeatable. |
| **Defense** | Stands flat-footed; no pursuit. | Ready position only when reminded; pursues without touching. | Ready position held through the opponent's contact on 8/10 rallies; touches 5/10 balls fed 5-8 ft away; calls "free" or "hit" correctly on 6/10. | Touches pursued balls consistently; free-ball-vs-attack call reliable and early. | Get-back / get-low / get-touch on a coach down-ball; hips under the ball on a hard-driven dig; calls the ball before it arrives. |
| **Transition** | Admires the ball after contact; no recovery. | Recovers slowly; no pass-set-hit shape. | Recovers to her ready spot on 8/10 rallies; completes her role in a cooperative pass-set-hit shape on 5/10 without freezing. | Pass-set-hit shape executed with teammates at speed; urgency between contacts visible. | Free-ball pass-set-hit executed cleanly in a cooperative drill; recognizes and responds to the free-ball call. |
| **Mental & coachability** | Does not attempt feedback; protests repetition. | Inconsistent attempts at feedback; tolerates repetition reluctantly. | Attempts the correction on the very next rep after 3 of 4 corrections; tolerates a full 10-minute repetition block; engages teammates positively. | Self-corrects within the rep cycle without a cue; asks clarifying questions. | Self-corrects between reps; repeats the cue back accurately; engages teammates' reps during off-rep time; FeedForward-receptive. |
| **Systems** | Cannot name her position; rotates the wrong way; no awareness of the lineup. | Names her position with help; rotation direction right about half the time; "stay or switch" not understood. | Names her own position in a simple 4-2 on 5/6 rotations; steps the correct direction after a side-out on 5/6 without help; answers "stay or switch?" correctly on 4/6. | Tracks her position through all six rotations unprompted; corrects a teammate's rotation error; explains why the switch happens, not just when. | Plays a position in a 6-2 without needing rotation help from the coach; calls overlap correctly when asked ("am I behind or in front of my setter?"). |

## Scoring

Per athlete, record one score (1-5) on each of the **nine** criteria. Total = 9-45.

**Promotion gate to [[12s]], stated identically here and in [[11s]] `## Promotion criteria`:**

> An 11s athlete promotes to [[12s]] on **3 or higher on at least 7 of the 9 rubric rows, with no score of 1 in Passing or Serving.**

The row count is the gate; the total-score bands below are a reading aid, not a second gate. Where the count and the band disagree, the count decides.

- **Total 36-45 (avg 4.0+):** Comfortably past the gate. Flag for the higher-team conversation described under Column 5.
- **Total 27-35 (avg 3.0-3.9):** The on-track band. Most 11s graduates land here, and they promote.
- **Total 18-26 (avg 2.0-2.9):** Repeat 11s recommended. Multiple criteria not yet at age-appropriate level. Continue 11U development on the lowest 2-3 criteria.
- **Total 9-17 (avg <2.0):** Reassess fit. Either a developmental support gap, calendar-age mismatch (a younger 10U-aligned athlete), or pre-introduction-phase. Discuss with the parent and explore [[10s]] placement or a developmental track.

A single 5 does not promote and a single 1 does not demote, **with one exception**: a score of 1 in Passing or Serving blocks promotion regardless of the other eight rows. Those two carry the load-bearing 11U-specific milestones (recognizable platform; the overhand-serve attempt), and an athlete without either has no way to participate in a 12s practice. Passing and Serving are also the tiebreak rows for any borderline call.

## Calibration notes

**Session design.** Run the rubric across a 60-90-minute session: warm-up + footwork (10 min, scores movement quality and pursuit signals); passing + serving stations (20 min, scores Passing + Serving); setting + hitting stations (20 min, scores Setting + Hitting); blocking + defense (10 min, scores Blocking + Defense); **6v6 in the 4-2** (15-20 min, scores Transition, Mental & coachability, and **Systems**). The last block has to be 6v6, not 4v4, because the Systems row asks about position in a six-rotation 4-2, and a 4v4 court cannot show you that. Run the earlier small-sided work at 4v4 if you like; the Systems row is evaluated in the 6v6 block only.

**Train what you score.** The Blocking and Defense rows are only fair if the team has rehearsed them. [[11s-drills]] carries [[block-footwork-ladder]] (shuffle and crossover patterns only at this age) and [[munciana-speedball]] for read-and-run defense. An athlete scored on a skill she was never given reps at is being scored on her out-of-season background, not on your coaching.

**Checkpoint re-scoring.** These nine rows are re-scored at week 6 and week 12 per [[11s]] `## Season operations`. Use this document unchanged at those checkpoints.

**Inter-rater agreement.** With 2+ evaluators, score independently then compare. Discrepancies of 2+ points trigger a re-watch (or re-rep) before resolving — never average without discussion. Anchor each evaluator on Column 3 ("consistent at age-appropriate level") as the calibration baseline before tryouts begin.

**Common biases to avoid.**
- **Size bias:** taller 11U athletes look more "12s-ready" but vertical reach is not a rubric criterion at this age. Score the skill, not the body.
- **Multi-sport athlete bias:** athletes with limited volleyball reps but strong general athleticism may score 2-3 on technique while showing 4-5 movement quality. Per [[11s]] athletic profile, multi-sport play is evidence-backed injury prevention; do not penalize for it.
- **Loud-energy bias:** the quiet self-correcting athlete often outranks the loud one on Mental & coachability. Score on rep-by-rep behavior, not volume.
- **Cue-language over-engineering:** Moran et al. 2023 found no significant cue-type advantage in youth populations. Do not score down an athlete who responds to a different cue family — score the resulting motor behavior.

**Forward link.** Athletes clearing the gate graduate into the [[12s]] context, where the platform must become stable, the standing-float serve becomes the default, the offense graduates from the 11s 4-2 install to a simple [[offense-6-2]] by season's end, and overhead passing is added on free balls. Athletes scoring 5 on most rows are already at the 12s column-3 standard. That is the trigger for a higher-team or accelerated-grouping conversation, and it is not a reason to teach 12s content in an 11s gym.
