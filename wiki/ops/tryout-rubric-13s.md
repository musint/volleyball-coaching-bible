# Tryout Rubric: 13s

## Purpose

This rubric translates the [[13s]] `## Promotion criteria` section into a 1-5 scoring scale for tryout evaluation. Use it for athletes coming **into** a 13s team (returning 12s graduates, lateral-move 13s athletes) and for end-of-season 13s evaluations that determine 14U-team placement. The rubric pairs with [[age-lens-14u]] as the parent club lens. The 13s-specific gate is the **serving row**: in-court reliability plus in-called-zone reliability, two separable numbers, and it is the milestone that distinguishes a 13s graduate from a 12s graduate. Topspin variants stay 16+ in this program.

**Column 5 of every row is the next age's column-3 standard; it flags readiness, it is not install content for this age.** Each column-5 cell below is written against the [[tryout-rubric-14u]] column-3 anchors so the two rubrics dovetail at the seam. A 5 is a placement signal about where the athlete already is, not permission to teach 14s content to the 13s group.

Pass grades use the 0-3 scale whose anchors are defined once at [[passing-serve-receive]].

## Evaluation criteria

Eight criteria across rows × five score levels across columns. Score each athlete on each row independently. A `5` on any row signals next-age-ready (that is, 14U-entry-ready specialization onset) for that skill.

Two structural changes from earlier versions of this page: the two serving rows that used to disagree with each other are now **one** row carrying two separable numbers, and the old catch-all "mental, system, athletic" row is now **two** rows (Systems, and Mental & athletic) because they measure unrelated things and were producing a single score that meant nothing.

| Criterion | 1 (does not demonstrate) | 2 (occasional) | 3 (consistent at 13s level) | 4 (above 13s) | 5 (14s-ready = [[tryout-rubric-14u]] column 3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Passing** (Kiraly 4 keys: stopped & balanced, thumbs together, set-the-angle, simple one-way motion; Kordes shuffle-not-reach) | Reach-passes; off-balance contact; arm-swing dominant. | 4 keys appear ≤3/10; thumbs occasionally split; partial shuffle. | ≥6/10 medium-pace serves to 6-ft target with platform-not-arm-swing; pass-grade logged alongside the fraction. | ≥7/10; quiet platform on hard serves; pass-and-cover audible. | ≥60% in-system (grade 2+) off moderate-pace standing-float; quiet platform with the hop-back on jam balls; pass-and-cover sprint after contact; forearm-vs-overhead read is deliberate. |
| **Setting** (hand-hinge, side-of-ball contact, vision-on-target) | Catches every set; no hand-hinge. | Hand-hinge intermittent; thumbs-down or finger-flat contact. | Hand-hinge live (not catch-and-set); front-set to outside antenna ≥6/10. | Back-set without disguise; consistent location off off-target passes; jump-set introduced and repped in controlled drills. | Hand-hinge with side-of-ball contact; outside set hittable from an in-system pass; backset competent without telegraphing; Tempo 3 footwork demonstrable on tossed balls. |
| **Hitting** (3-step approach, Schmidt start-slow / accelerate-the-last-two; Mattox Sight-90 / Elbow-Lead / Lift-and-Whip; high-hand torque) | Two-foot stand-and-swing; arm-only contact. | 3-step approach inconsistent; flat or one-foot finish. | 3-step rhythmic; both feet under hitter at finish; high-elbow audible. | Closes the heel; opens shoulders to seam; line/cross/tip vocabulary live; back-row attack repped in controlled drills (Barnard's minimal entry). | Three-step approach with visible start-slow / accelerate-the-last-two tempo; elbow-lead and high-hand contact; at least two of {big cross, line, tip} usable. |
| **Blocking** (footwork-first; static-and-shuffle; Rosen 10-foot-line swing-block install, steps without jump) | No net presence; lunges across body. | Hands at net; shuffle inconsistent; penetration absent. | Static-and-shuffle clean; hand-press; partial penetration; **no jump on the swing-block pattern: footwork only is the 13s standard and adding the jump early is not credit**. | Outside-foot-forward closing stance audible in her own language; Rosen swing-block footwork without jump reliable at speed. | Static-and-shuffle reliable at the pin; outside-foot-forward closing; hand-press with net penetration; stick or step-back landing default, never run-back. (The middles' jump-add belongs at 14s and the pins' at late 15s; neither is scored here.) |
| **Serving** (Mattox Lift / Step / Swing; Dunning toss-first; Munciana aim-small-miss-small): **the 13s gate, two separable numbers** | Underhand only, or chronic net and long errors. | Standing float in-court ≤5/10; no zone awareness; toss inconsistent. | **In-court ≥7/10** from the 13s service line **and in-called-zone ≥6/10** on the same attempts; toss shoulder-height in front of the hitting shoulder; shoulder-square mechanics. Score the lower of the two numbers. | In-court ≥8/10 and in-zone ≥7/10; mixes deep and short targets; five-before-ten aggressive mentality visible. *Bonus line, not a scoring input:* jump-float toss consistent in isolation. | Jump-float in rotation with toss consistency as the gate rather than contact; standing-float retained as the in-zone fallback (≥60% to target zone); names the spot before every serve. *Bonus line:* jump-float in court ≥4/10 marks the top-club install target of 1-2 rotation servers by season's end. |
| **Defense & reading** (Munciana Three P's: posture / position / passion, glossed as get-back / get-low / get-touch; Stone hips-under-ball; Josephson Look / See / Decide) | Reach-only digs; eyes lock on the ball. | Three P's intermittent; coach-prompted reading only. | Three P's visible on hard-driven and on tip; hips-under-ball platform; eyes track setter then hitter on coach prompt. | Self-prompted Look/See/Decide; verbalizes what she saw post-rally. | Three P's habitual rather than coach-prompted; Look/See/Decide read on shot-vs-swing before contact; platform forms late, not early. |
| **Systems** (position identity; runs 6-2 without a whiteboard; 5-1 reps banked for setter candidates) | Loses her rotation; cannot name a base position; no positional preference or aptitude visible. | Rotation tracking inconsistent; needs the whiteboard; position preference stated but not matched by aptitude. | Runs the 6-2 without a whiteboard; knows her base position and spots overlap risk before the whistle. | Setter candidates have banked a month of 5-1 reps; non-setters can name their attacking responsibility inside the 5-1 across all six rotations. | Position identity emerged and holds up under load: a primary lane (setter / OH / libero / MB) is visible to an evaluator who has never seen her before, and she runs the team's system without needing the rotation explained. |
| **Mental & athletic** (FeedForward-receptive; one-cue self-correct; emergent self-talk; landing mechanics) | Deflects a "caught you doing it right" callout or brushes off a correction instead of acting on it; landings collapse inward at the knee. | Self-corrects only after multiple cues; landing quality mixed between reps. | One-cue self-correct; bilateral landings show knees over toes with no valgus collapse. | Self-talk vocabulary present and used unprompted; unilateral landings clean; stick-or-step-back is her default. | Self-corrects after one cue rather than three; effort holds independent of the score; communicates seams, switches and balls in-rally; stays engaged during teammates' reps. |

## Scoring

Each criterion scored 1-5 across eight rows. **Total possible: 40 (8 rows × 5).** Every row is scored for every athlete, because there is no position-conditional N/A on this page, which is deliberate, because 13s is the year you are still looking for position identity rather than confirming it.

**The 14U-entry profile.** Position-conditional bonuses are not added. Instead, a 5 on the **position-defining** row (Setting for setters, Hitting for OH/MB, Defense & reading for libero candidates) plus ≥3 on every other row is the standard 14U-entry profile.

**The serving gate.** The Serving row must score ≥3 for a 13s graduate, and it scores on the **lower** of the two numbers. An athlete who is in-court 9/10 but in-zone 4/10 scores against the 4. Sub-3 on this row keeps an athlete 13s-track regardless of anything else on the sheet.

**Row averages.** Where you need a single figure to compare athletes or to track a mid-season re-score, use the row average (total ÷ 8) rather than the raw total, so the number stays comparable against [[tryout-rubric-14u]] and [[tryout-rubric-15s]], which both band on averages.

## Calibration notes

**Session length.** A 60-90-minute tryout is the design envelope. Allocate roughly: 10 min dynamic warm-up + landing-mechanics observation; 15 min ball-control / passing; 10 min setting; 15 min hitting & approach; 10 min serving (standing float plus jump-float toss-only, the float-in-zone gate must be observed live); 15 min defense / reading; 15 min 6-2 / 5-1 small-sided (5-1 reps targeted at setter candidates).

**Multiple evaluators.** Score independently, then reconcile. Inter-rater agreement target: ≤1-point discrepancy on ≥6 of 8 criteria. Where evaluators diverge ≥2 points, default to the lower score and revisit on a second tryout day.

**Common biases to avoid.** (a) Maturity-blindness, pre-PHV athletes look weaker on power criteria but may have better coordination; weight the technique criteria, not the velocity. (b) Returning-athlete halo, score what you see today, not last season. (c) Setter-vacancy bias, do not promote a non-setter into the setter lane just because the team needs one. (d) Float-in-zone clemency, the gate is the gate; an athlete whose float will not land in a called zone is 13s-track for another season, regardless of how strong the rest of her serving looks. (e) Penalty-rep contamination: Munciana-style accountability drills create pressure organically; do not stack coach-imposed penalties on top. (f) Rewarding the early jump: a 13s blocker who has added the jump to her swing-block before the footwork is automatic is ahead of the curriculum, not ahead of her peers; score the feet.

**No projection.** Score what the gym shows today. Do not write future-position labels into a score. "Libero-track," "setter-track," "she'll be a middle" are notes for the evaluator panel, not rubric content, and this page deliberately no longer carries them in its column-5 anchors. What Systems column 5 asks is whether a *primary lane is already visible to a stranger*, which is an observation; what it does not ask is which lane you think she will end up in, which is a prediction.

**Position-identity emergence.** A 5 on the position-defining row plus ≥3 elsewhere is the 14U-entry profile. Multi-position viability remains a positive, see [[age-lens-14u]] on generalized specialists.
