# Tryout rubric — 16U

> Eight-row, five-column observable rubric for evaluating 16U club volleyball tryouts. Column 3 is the 16s standard ([[16s]] promotion criteria, mirrored). Column 5 is 17s-ready (early advanced markers — flag for 17U-or-above placement). Use alongside [[age-lens-hs]] (parent context) and [[recruiting]] (16s is the recruiting auto-evaluation year).

## Purpose

The 16U tryout evaluates against the same milestones the 16s age-guide says a player must demonstrate to graduate to 17s. Eight skill areas, five-point scale, observable cells. Specialization-phase year — every athlete should arrive with a position identity (libero / OH / MB / S / Opp), at least one HS varsity season behind her, and a settled offensive system. The rubric does the recruiting-relevant first-pass: which athletes are 16s-standard, which are 17s-ready, and which need targeted development to hold a roster spot. Pair with the matched [[16s]] age-guide for the underlying milestone rationale.

**Entry-calibrated rows.** The serving and back-row-attack criteria are calibrated to what a candidate can show on **tryout day**, before the 16s year has taught her anything. Topspin is introduced at 16s by program design, so no entering candidate has it, and the bottom half of most 15s rosters is still serving standing-float. Season-exit standards for those two rows (standing-topspin contact clean, jump-topspin in rotation, back-row kill rate) live in the [[16s]] promotion table, not here. Every other row is calibrated the same at entry and exit.

## Evaluation criteria

| Skill | 1: not yet | 2: occasional | 3: 16s standard | 4: above 16s | 5: 17s-ready |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Passing** | Pass-grade <1.5 in 6v6; mishandles routine floats | Pass-grade 1.5-1.8; misses libero-split read on jump-floats | Pass-grade ~2.0; libero-split serviceable [[serve-receive-libero-split]]; calls early/loud | Pass-grade 2.2+; reads server toss-and-shoulder pre-contact | Pass-grade 2.4+ vs. varsity jump-topspin; OH-2 backup-libero capable (passes 2.0+ across a full libero rotation block) |
| **Setting** | Stand-sets only; middle quick fails | Jump-sets sometimes; middle quick fails on imperfect pass | Jump-sets reliably; runs Tempo 1/2/3 on graded passes; basic OOS hittable 50%+ | Disguised backsets; OOS hittable 70%+; calls audibles | OOS hittable 80%+; reads block before set; in-rotation tempo adjustment |
| **Hitting** | One shot only; approach inconsistent; misses approach reps | Two shots called pre-swing; back-row attempt with low success | 4-step approach mature; 3+ shots; pipe entry executed with correct takeoff behind the 10-foot line | Closes-heel consistently; reads block; tip/roll/cut as live tools; back-row 30%+ kill rate vs. single block | Back-row 40%+ vs. double; one-foot landing recovery; recruiting-grade approach-touch |
| **Blocking** | Square only; tooling room; runs back to land | Swing-block emerging; inconsistent timing; some controlled landings | Swing-block footwork mature [[blocking-swing]]; can explain when bunch-read beats spread; step-back default | Reads hitter pre-set; commit-block timing; seam-close from the outside-foot-forward pin stance | Reads setter eyes; in-rotation block-scheme calls; right-side cross-court block |
| **Serving** | Standing float misses the called zone more often than it hits; high error rate | Standing float in-zone in blocked reps (~70%) but breaks down in 6v6; jump-float attempted, not live | Standing float lands in the called zone for every candidate; jump-float live for candidates who carried top-half serving usage on a 15s roster; called-zone accuracy holds at ≥60% in 6v6 | Early adopter: standing-topspin contact clean from the end line; ace rate ≥8%; missed-serve discipline visible | Jump-topspin live in rotation at match error rate under 15%; serves to scout; in-match toss-adjust |
| **Defense** | Reactive; chases ball; out of position on tips | Reads hitter sometimes; pursuit OK on routine | Reads hitter approach + arm; libero-in-6 default [[defense-middle-back]]; pursuit chains 2 contacts | Reads setter eyes; calls coverage adjustments; emergency dig recovery | Reads setter + hitter; OOS dig-to-pass consistent; libero overhead second-ball |
| **Transition** | Block-to-approach broken; OOS bails fail | Transition swing on clean digs only; setter second-ball reactive | Block-to-approach in 3 steps; bail-to-pin reliable; OOS-to-attack live | First-ball-kill in transition; setter second-ball protocol; pipe transition | OOS-attack 30%+ kill rate; in-rotation transition adjust; libero overhead set |
| **Mental & coachability** | Inconsistent effort; defensive on feedback | Solid effort; takes feedback; emotional on errors | Chooses the scored side of optional drills; tracks and announces her own drill score; resets teammates after errors; states a position identity and defends it with her own skill profile | Constructive self-talk audible after an error; identical pre-serve routine across ten consecutive serves; teammate accountability | Recruiting-grade competitor; full mental-skills toolkit; leadership behaviors |

## Scoring

Each evaluator scores all 8 rows independently per athlete. Sum the row scores for an aggregate (max 40). Apply the team-decision filter:

- **Roster-bid 17s-ready:** column 5 in 4+ rows AND no row below column 3. Flag for 17U placement consideration; if 16U is the only roster, she anchors it.
- **Roster-bid 16s standard:** column 3 across all 8 rows OR column 4 in 4+ rows with column 2 minimum elsewhere. This is the promotion bar from the [[16s]] age-guide and the default 16U roster offer.
- **Development bid:** column 2 in 2-3 rows with column 3 elsewhere. Roster-edge case — discuss in evaluator panel; consider position-specific development plan.
- **No bid:** column 1 in 2+ rows OR aggregate below the no-bid floor. Recommend 15U or club-development-team placement; communicate to family per [[club-ops-parent-comms-templates]].

The setter row applies only to athletes auditioning for setter. Non-setters score the other 7 rows, maximum 35. Both threshold sets, stated rather than left to the evaluator to prorate:

| Decision | Setter candidates (8 rows, max 40) | Non-setters (7 rows, max 35) |
|---|---|---|
| Roster-bid 17s-ready | Column 5 in 4+ rows, no row below column 3 | Column 5 in 4+ rows, no row below column 3 |
| Roster-bid 16s standard | Column 3 in all 8 rows, or column 4 in 4+ rows with column 2 minimum elsewhere | Column 3 in all 7 rows, or column 4 in 4+ rows with column 2 minimum elsewhere |
| Development bid | Column 2 in 2-3 rows, column 3 elsewhere | Column 2 in 2-3 rows, column 3 elsewhere |
| No-bid aggregate floor | Aggregate <18 | Aggregate <16 |

## Calibration notes

**Session structure (90 min target).** 10 min warm-up observation (athleticism + work ethic baseline). 20 min skill stations (passing wave + setting wave + hitting lines + serve/receive). 25 min position-specific (setter audit, libero pursuit, MB block-and-attack, OH back-row). 30 min controlled 6v6 with scoring rules that punish casual play [aoc-2026-rose-no-free-balls] (giveaway free balls = -1, missed serves = -2, back-row kills count double). 5 min cooldown + flex/jump-touch measure.

**Inter-rater agreement.** Three evaluators minimum; each scores independently before any panel discussion. After session, compare rows where evaluators disagree by 2+ points and re-watch the relevant clip if recorded. The bunch-read [aoc-2026-bunch-read-blocking] and libero-split [aoc-2024-sr-formations] reads are evaluator-skill-dependent — flag any evaluator whose blocking/passing scores diverge systematically from the panel mean for calibration video work pre-tryout.

**Common biases to avoid.** Recency-bias on the last drill of the session (rotate evaluation order). Approach-touch halo (a 9'8" touch is a recruiting datum, not a skill column). Familiarity-bias toward returning athletes vs. tryout-only candidates (score the rep observed, not the player history). Single-rep panic (column 3 means "consistent at age-appropriate level" — one mishit doesn't drop a column). Pressure-acclimatization gap [mesagno-2017-choking-models-interventions] — first-time tryout athletes may underperform their true level; offer a shorter return-block on day 2 if scheduling allows.

**Recruiting hand-off.** Column 5 athletes are recruiting-relevant; flag for [[recruiting]] timeline check (NCAA D1 contact opens June 15 after sophomore year). Approach-touch, standing reach, and jump-touch numbers logged pre-tryout-day with handheld jump-touch device — these are recruiting-card data and belong in the athlete file regardless of bid.

**Self-talk and pressure routine [walter-2019-self-talk-junior-athletes].** Mental-row column 4 scores the *output* of a self-talk install, not attendance at one: constructive self-talk audible after an error, and an identical pre-serve routine held across ten consecutive serves. Score what you see on tryout day. A returning athlete who completed the program's 8-week block but shows neither behavior does not get the column; a first-year candidate who shows both does.

**Serving-row zone protocol.** *Called-zone accuracy* means the evaluator names one of the six service zones before each serve and the server lands in the named zone. Column 3 asks for ≥60% inside 6v6, not inside a blocked serving block. Run at least twelve called serves per candidate before scoring the row.

**Position identity.** Fifteens certifies a settled position identity (libero / OH / MB / S / Opp); the 16U tryout verifies it rather than re-opening it. Ask the candidate directly. A candidate who cannot name her position and tie the choice to her own skill profile scores column 2 on the mental row whatever else the row shows.
