# Tryout rubric: 18u

## Purpose

This rubric translates the [[18s]] `## Promotion criteria` into a graded 1-5 evaluation tool for an 18u tryout. The 18u year is the **college bridge**: see [[age-lens-college]] for the parent context. The unifying frame: column 5 ("college-ready") is calibrated to "would survive a college preseason on August 1," not "completed an 18s season." For recruiting-active athletes, [[recruiting]] adds a parallel evaluation layer (target-list discipline, film standards, signing-date awareness) that runs alongside the on-court rubric.

The rubric reads against an 80-90 minute combined tryout session: 20 min warm-up + skill-station rotation, 30 min controlled drill (serve-receive + transition + 6v6 wash), 30 min open scrimmage with end-game pressure rules, 10 min interview + measurement station (height / wingspan / standing reach / approach jump touch / vertical).

## Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | 1: Does not demonstrate | 2: Occasional | 3: Consistent at age | 4: Above age | 5: College-ready |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Passing** | Cannot reliably platform jump-float; no seam communication. | Platforms jump-float; jump-topspin passes drift; seam calls absent. | **2.0+ tournament pass average** in libero-split vs. jump-float and slow topspin; pre-serve seam call audible before every serve. | Passes high-velocity topspin in libero-split at 2.0+; calls the four-passer adjustment in-rotation without a coach prompt. | 2.0+ rating vs. college-tier topspin; pre-serve seam ownership instant; pass grades tied to **what offense ran**. |
| **Setting** | 5-1 only in three rotations; back-set absent; OOS dumps. | 5-1 across six rotations; jump-set inconsistent; OOS one-handed. | Full 5-1; jump-set from Zone 2/3; OOS two-handed redirect functional. | Back-set disguise; tempo-call audible (T1/T2/T3); late-match rotational drift identified **on video** and correcting inside the same match. | Real-time tempo audibles; OOS two-handed consistent; rotational-component repeatability sustained under fatigue, video-verified across a tournament day rather than eyeballed from the bench. |
| **Hitting** | 3-step approach; arm-pull contact; in-system only. | 4-step approach; high-hand inconsistent; line-only or angle-only toolkit. | Modern 4-step mature; line/angle/tip in-system; back-row Zone 6 reliable. | Cut/roll/seam shots; OOS swing toolkit; reads block-and-defender pre-contact. | College-tier OOS swing; approach-velocity and penultimate-step trained as personal levers; Zones 6/1 in-system reliable. |
| **Blocking** | Reach-blocking; run-back landings default. | Read-blocking footwork; arm-swing penetration intermittent. | Swing-blocking footwork operational; bunch-read overlay installed and run as the default (it has been the default since 16s); step-back default; controlled-stick landing. | Full bunch-read variant tree live (multiple quick tempos, cross-court pin support, rotation-specific pinch depth); commit situationally vs. dominant middles. | Variant selected off a scouted tendency before the serve; hand-press penetration; landing mechanics audited on video every set; commit-call instant. |
| **Serving** | Standing-float only or unreliable jump-float. | Jump-float in; aim drifts; jump-topspin attempted but inconsistent. | Jump-float reliable to two called zones; aim-small framing internalized; ace rate and miss rate both tracked. | Jump-topspin a reliable change-up at match error rate under 15%; ace/miss balance internalized; pressure-rehearsal evident. | Jump-topspin a recruiting-relevant differentiator: called against a scouted passer in a named rotation and holding its error rate there; approach-velocity refined; five-before-ten architecture rehearsed and applied. |
| **Defense** | Reactive ball-watching; no rotation scheme. | Rotation-specific scheme partial; pursuit footwork breaks down. | Reads the hitter's approach and arm, not the ball; libero-in-6 default; Three P's audible. | Reads setter release and hitter pre-contact; calls rotation-specific adjustments; pursuit-and-recovery automatic; late-match valgus drift correcting. | Rotation-one tactical levers (bunch-read / commit / shading / short-serve disrupt) callable; third-set landing form matches first-set. |
| **Transition** | Block lands and stops; dig-to-approach undecided; OOS bail absent. | Block-to-approach inconsistent; setter second-ball protocol breaks down. | Block-to-approach automatic; OOS detect-call-bail functional; setter second-ball clean. | Dig-to-approach pre-decided; libero second-ball overhead from behind 3m operational. | Third and fourth transition rallies executed at the same quality as the first; OOS-to-pin instant; setter second-ball clinical. |
| **Mental / coachability** | Blames; freelances role; quiet under pressure. | Accepts feedback post-drill; pre-performance routine absent. | Ownership audible; self-correction within drill; explicit role-acceptance. | Self-talk and imagery scaffolded; pressure routines visible and holding under fatigue; all four pressure interventions programmed. | Championship behaviors operational [mccutcheon-2022-championship-behaviors]; 8-week self-talk install evident; pressure-routine durable; superpower self-defined. |

## Scoring

Each evaluator scores all eight criteria independently 1-5, then a position-specific weighting yields the composite:

- **Setter:** setting × 2, defense × 1.5, all others × 1.
- **Libero / DS:** passing × 2, defense × 2, all others × 1, blocking × 0.5, hitting × 0.5.
- **OH / Opp:** hitting × 2, passing × 1.5 (OH only), all others × 1.
- **MB:** blocking × 2, hitting × 1.5, transition × 1.5, passing × 0.5.

Composite cuts: **40+ college-ready**; **32-39 returning 18s starter**; **24-31 development-track**; **<24 misplacement** (consider 17s).

Athletic-profile measurements (the physical card: height, wingspan, standing reach, approach jump touch, vertical, per [[usav-nd-college-recruitment-timeline]]) are recorded but not scored. They feed [[recruiting]] conversations and the tier-benchmark table on [[18s]], not the on-court composite.

**Burnout monitoring is a coach responsibility, not a scored row.** The volleyball burnout pattern across a long season is erosion of sense-of-accomplishment and rising sport devaluation while exhaustion stays flat, so it will not show up as a bad tryout score. Watch for it separately and continuously; do not ask an athlete to self-report it into a rubric cell.

## Calibration notes

**Inter-rater agreement.** Use 2-3 evaluators; average independent scores; flag any criterion with >2-point spread for live review. The 1-5 anchors are cell-text-specific, read the column header, not the number, when scoring.

**Tryout-session arc.** Warm-up + skill-station scores criteria in isolation (passing, setting, hitting, serving). Controlled drill scores blocking, defense, transition under cooperative load. Scrimmage with end-game pressure (Munciana Biggie Smalls / Prove It / Finish Strong style) scores mental/coachability and stress-tests every other criterion [munciana-2022-camp-drills]. Mental score weights scrimmage observation 70 / interview 30.

**Common biases.**
- **Halo from height.** Tall MBs default-score 4 on blocking; force the cell-text read (penetration, hand-press, landing).
- **Recency bias.** Last-rally errors anchor whole-tryout impressions; require evaluators to fill the rubric in two passes (mid-session + end).
- **Recruiting halo.** A committed athlete is not automatically a 5; column 5 is observable execution, not commitment status.
- **Familiarity bias.** Returning club athletes default-score higher; calibrate against the cell text, not against last year.

**Recruiting overlay.** For the recruiting-active subset, [[recruiting]] criteria (target-list discipline, film standards, coach-communication, camp investment, signing-date awareness, women's volleyball single initial signing date 2025-26 is November 12, 2025) run as a parallel evaluation. A college-ready scorecard with a chaotic recruiting process is a coachable gap, not a tryout-failure gap.
