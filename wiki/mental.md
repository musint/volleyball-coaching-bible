# Mental

## Overview

"Mental" is the catch-all for everything that shapes how a team behaves under load: culture, leadership, pressure, motivation, adversity, communication, and the routines that make those behaviors repeatable. On the skill spine it is a **connective** skill. It owns no contact of its own and shows up inside every other one, at the service line, in the seam call, in the swing she takes at 22-24.

Modern coaching treats mental as a set of trainable, observable behaviors you design into the program the same way you design serve receive or a blocking scheme. The instrumentation followed: the MTQ48 drew factorial-validity scrutiny that produced 18-item Short and 6-item Very Short refinements, so longitudinal monitoring no longer costs a survey session [kawabata-2021-mtq48-refinement].

Five framings anchor the area. McCutcheon's *Championship Behaviors* is the flagship: championship results require championship behaviors, coaches teach and mentor while athletes work and compete, and the living expression is the competitive cauldron he built at Minnesota with Laurie Eisler. Kiraly works through culture and curiosity. Rose's "everything matters" is the operational version. Liskevych owns the emotional layer, arguing you control your own feelings before asking that of players. Dunning treats culture as a deliberate coaching output, and Hebert's *Thinking Volleyball* supplies the classical mental-model-of-play lens.

**Scope.** Everything here is coaching psychology. Performance anxiety, confidence, motivation, and role acceptance sit inside your job. Clinical anxiety, depression, disordered eating, and trauma sit outside it. When what you are seeing extends past the gym and into an athlete's life, the move is a referral to a licensed professional and a conversation with her family.

**Operational counterpart.** [[mental-skills-curriculum]] is the dosage layer: which mental skill goes in this week's plan, at which age, in what quantity, with what cue. This hub answers why and when; that page answers how much and how.

## Major subtopics

Pointers only. Research detail lives on [[mental-skills-curriculum]]; per-year application lives in the age-guides.

- **Team culture**: a coaching output. Rudiger's three pillars are the most portable club version, holding equal treatment (respect, expectation, and developmental opportunity constant across the depth chart), family (belonging paired with accountability), and a stated work-ethic expectation [aoc-2025-rudiger-program-culture]. Cohesion drives collective efficacy at elite level only, and task cohesion outweighs social cohesion [spink-1990-cohesion-collective-efficacy-volleyball].
- **Pressure and performance**: choking splits into attention-based and self-presentation models, with intervention support for pressure acclimatization, routines, attentional cueing, and quiet-eye [mesagno-2017-choking-models-interventions]. Volleyball-specific tests temper the dogma, since routine presence alone did not improve club serving accuracy [billing-2014-pre-performance-routines-volleyball] and "don't serve into the net" produced no ironic-error spike [gorgulu-2021-avoiding-instructions-volleyball-pressure].
- **Leadership, coach side**: **perceived** support (she believes help is available) predicts self-confidence, while **received** support (help actually delivered) predicts wellbeing, both through coach-athlete relationship quality [coussens-2024-coach-support-confidence-wellbeing]. Relationship first, then both levers.
- **Leadership, player side**: captains and in-play leaders carry standards between your interventions. Wise's superpower frame gives each athlete a defined strength to lead from [wise-2023-characterandleadership-profile].
- **Motivation and mental toughness**: coaches train competitive behavior, athletes own toughness. Trinsey separates work (produces an outcome), play (done for its own sake), and competition (a judged outcome), noting that many teenagers now read "play" as Saturday's tournament [trinsey-2026-work-play-compete-part-1]. His rule is to be more demanding in practice and more supportive in competition.
- **Communication and post-game**: [[aoc-2026-post-game-talk]] is the operational reference, with the iY-generation piece as the feedback-reception layer [aoc-2018-coaching-iy-generation]. Self-talk works as an 8-week block and fails as a one-shot [walter-2019-self-talk-junior-athletes]; imagery needs scripts, prompts, and vividness training to be coachable [pourmohseni-2025-imagery-collegiate-volleyball-season].
- **Adversity and post-loss recovery**: Rose's excellence-versus-success distinction treats losses as information. Volleyball burnout is erosion of meaning, since reduced accomplishment and sport devaluation rose across a 10-month season while exhaustion stayed flat [pires-2021-burnout-coping-volleyball-season].

## Teaching by age band

Per-year precision lives in the age-guides and tryout rubrics. These cells point there rather than restating them.

### 10-12: Foundation

- **Introduce:** love of the game as the outcome metric. Coach-modeled affect is the delivery mechanism, so you say "good ball, next one" out loud after a miss, hers or yours. FeedForward and "catch them doing it right" are the feedback defaults through 11U [usav-2026-simplified-youth-rules]. Teach play as its own category, since many kids arrive needing to learn how to play [trinsey-2026-work-play-compete-part-1].
- **Develop:** mistake response inside a rep cycle, where rep N+1 attempts the correction. Tolerance for repetition. Live-target serving reps before any verbal placement rule, since implicit-then-explicit ordering beat both alternatives in matched 10 to 12 year-old female novices [lola-2012-implicit-explicit-volleyball-serving]. Anxious athletes here are under-repped rather than under-talented [aoc-2025-cc-skill-training-clubwide].
- **Refine:** nothing instrumented. Routines lack a closed-skill substrate, self-talk blocks eat the attention budget [walter-2019-self-talk-junior-athletes], toughness instruments have no use [kawabata-2021-mtq48-refinement]. Skip cue-type optimization: neutral control matched or beat external, internal, and analogy cues on youth jump and sprint [moran-2023-verbal-coaching-cues-youth].
- **Year gates:** **10s**, no penalty-heavy wash drills, since anxiety on an unstable motor program produces a player who fears the ball. **11s**, asking questions reads as coachability. **12s**, lowest-stakes Munciana psychology games (Biggie Smalls, Prove It, Finish Strong) enter with short games, low-cost penalties, fast resets [munciana-2022-camp-drills].
- **Promotion criterion (observable):** inside one rep block she shanks three balls and is back in ready position before the next contact each time, with no change in posture or footwork speed.
- **Links:** [[10s]], [[11s]], [[12s]], [[10s-drills]], [[11s-drills]], [[12s-drills]].

### 13-14: Rebuild

- **Introduce:** identity, role acceptance, and the first real pressure routine. Build the pre-serve routine as four components (breath, two ball bounces, eyes lock target, one cue word) against Bechard's criteria: repeatable above all, simple over complex, a minimal-motion toss, and a mental component such as a breath or repeated phrase [aoc-2026-bechard-serving-routine]. Coach the components, since routine presence by itself did not improve club serving accuracy [billing-2014-pre-performance-routines-volleyball][mesagno-2017-choking-models-interventions]. Grade consistency, never accuracy.
- **Role-setting before tournaments:** state roles, playing time, and expectations explicitly, leaning slightly pessimistic over sugar-coating. People can stand to be disappointed and they hate to be surprised, so let her be pleasantly surprised instead [trinsey-2026-notes-from-orlando-part-1]. That conversation survives contact only where the culture standard is already decoupled from the depth chart [aoc-2025-rudiger-program-culture].
- **Develop:** effort independent of score, with the drill manufacturing the pressure ("every passing drill has a winner and a loser") [aoc-2026-lingenfelter-accountability-passing]. Intention checking as a diagnostic separate from instruction checking: ask "what were you trying to do there," and treat a footwork answer as the warning sign [trinsey-2026-good-intentions]. Athlete-owned self-talk under coach modeling at a 4-week intro dose [walter-2019-self-talk-junior-athletes]. External-focus cueing for arm-swing [slovak-2023-external-focus-volleyball-spike].
- **Refine:** implicit-then-explicit ordering from Foundation, now against tougher serves.
- **Not yet:** IZOF profiling, Short MTQ as a graded instrument, the full 8-week self-talk block, stacked sequential cues for setters [dearruda-2022-sequential-focus-volleyball-setting][nogueira-2019-izof-volleyball-anxiety][kawabata-2021-mtq48-refinement].
- **Year gates:** **13s**, self-talk installs as a season-long routine rather than a pre-tournament one-shot [walter-2019-self-talk-junior-athletes]. **14s**, self-feedback capability is the milestone separating older teams from younger ones [aoc-2025-cc-skill-training-clubwide].
- **Promotion criterion (observable):** the same four-component routine on her first serve of the day and her serve at 22-24 in the last match, no component dropped and no tempo change, across a tournament day. Second marker: she takes the pre-tournament role conversation, plays that role in match 1, and does not renegotiate on the bench.
- **Links:** [[13s]], [[14s]], [[age-lens-14u]], [[tryout-rubric-13s]], [[tryout-rubric-14u]], [[serving-cues]], [[serving]].

### 15-16: Refinement

- **Introduce:** competitive routines, arousal regulation, leadership development. Run the full 8-week self-talk block in preseason or early season, never tournament week [walter-2019-self-talk-junior-athletes]. All four supported anti-choking interventions become coachable at age dosing: pressure acclimatization, pre-performance routines, attentional cueing, quiet-eye [mesagno-2017-choking-models-interventions]. Add role-specific imagery scripts with fixed prompts at warm-up close-out and between sets [pourmohseni-2025-imagery-collegiate-volleyball-season].
- **Arousal regulation is individual from the start.** Optimal pre-match anxiety differed by 2 to 12 points across teammates, and within-zone matches outperformed out-of-zone for the same athlete, so a single team script is structurally wrong [nogueira-2019-izof-volleyball-anxiety]. Pressure format: Rose's end-of-set game, three games to 15 starting at 9-9, 11-11, and 13-13, coach initiating each rally to the team that just scored [aoc-2026-rose-end-of-set-pressure-game].
- **Develop:** hand over on-court decision authority and treat how she uses it as an evaluation [aoc-2024-kiraly-give-freedom]. Install process-versus-result vocabulary [aoc-2015-kiraly-process-vs-result]. The practice standard is "no free balls, we don't give them" plus compete-in-practice [aoc-2026-rose-no-free-balls][aoc-2023-kiraly-competing-in-practice], made countable by the earn-versus-give ledger [aoc-2026-theis-earn-vs-give-points]. Five-before-ten is the daily aggressive-mentality reset [munciana-2022-camp-drills].
- **Refine:** routine components from Rebuild, now monitored under real crowd noise. Cue load pruned to one per rep for skilled setters, since stacked sequential cues degraded skilled female setters' accuracy regardless of direction [dearruda-2022-sequential-focus-volleyball-setting]. Generalized-specialist self-concept [usav-2026-hp-clinic-lessons-shared].
- **Year gates:** **15s**, the milestone is sustained engagement with sport-psychology work. **16s**, first meaningful-crowd competition (state playoffs, USAV Junior National Qualifiers, AAU Nationals); IZOF profiling for starters across five or more matches; one-cue-per-rep becomes the setter rule; Short MTQ at season end as a returning-player tool [kawabata-2021-mtq48-refinement]; you become a recruiting touchpoint, since D1 women's coaches may first initiate contact June 15 after sophomore year [usav-nd-college-recruitment-timeline].
- **Promotion criterion (observable):** she runs her pre-serve routine unprompted at 22-24 in a third set with the crowd loud, complete and at first-serve tempo. Second marker: she takes a hard correction in front of teammates and the next rep shows the adjustment, with nothing defensive in her body language between the two.
- **Links:** [[15s]], [[16s]], [[age-lens-hs]], [[tryout-rubric-15s]], [[tryout-rubric-16u]], [[15s-drills]], [[16s-drills]], [[recruiting]].

### 17-18: Performance

- **Introduce:** autonomy, accountability, college-transition psychology. Multi-season burnout monitoring starts here, tracking reduced sense of accomplishment and sport devaluation across two or more club years, with confidence and motivation as the leading indicators, because volleyball burnout is erosion of meaning rather than fatigue [pires-2021-burnout-coping-volleyball-season]. The athlete who goes quiet at week 12 is the signal; visible exhaustion at week 20 is late. Very Short MTQ as an in-season check-in [kawabata-2021-mtq48-refinement]. Competitive cauldron as daily practice design [aoc-2013-mccutcheon-eisler-cauldron][aoc-2013-mccutcheon-coopetition].
- **Pull both support levers:** stating availability and delivering during stressors drive different outcomes [coussens-2024-coach-support-confidence-wellbeing]. Role clarity is the cohesion lever, well ahead of bonding events [spink-1990-cohesion-collective-efficacy-volleyball]. Every athlete leaves with a defined superpower, since an 18s who arrives at college without one struggles with role clarity inside her first month [wise-2023-characterandleadership-profile][aoc-2022-rose-coach-for-life].
- **Develop:** McCutcheon's responsibility split becomes operationally enforceable, with athletes owning work, learning, and competing while coaches own teaching, coaching, and mentoring [mccutcheon-2022-championship-behaviors]. Teaching the bench is the highest-yield move in a multi-day tournament, buying 15 seconds and a two-way exchange where an on-court athlete gets only a shout [trinsey-2026-notes-from-orlando-part-1]. Structure the post-game talk rather than improvising it [aoc-2026-post-game-talk].
- **Refine:** every prior-band tool at operational depth, programmed onto the season calendar. Routines rehearsed under fatigue. Imagery scripts the athlete edits monthly [pourmohseni-2025-imagery-collegiate-volleyball-season].
- **Year gates:** **17s**, pre-performance routines are the lowest-effort and highest-yield install for the college-bound athlete, and recruiters read coachability markers straight off the court at AAU Nationals. **18s**, Very Short MTQ every two weeks, and explicit role acceptance in scrimmage, with OH-2 playing OH-2 and nobody freelancing.
- **Promotion criterion (observable):** in the fifth set of the third match of a tournament day, under fatigue and unprompted, she runs her full pre-performance routine intact. Second marker: she names her own error out loud in real time, self-corrects inside the same drill window, and holds her assignment through a scoring run against her.
- **Links:** [[17s]], [[18s]], [[age-lens-college]], [[tryout-rubric-17s]], [[tryout-rubric-18u]], [[tryout-rubric-college-walkon]], [[recruiting]].

## Schools of thought

- [[art-of-coaching-volleyball]]: arguably the richest mental-work corpus in coach education, built from Kiraly, Rose, Dunning, Liskevych, and McCutcheon content. Culture, communication, and competing-in-practice are foreground content.
- [[gold-medal-squared]]: less explicit culture language, with competing-in-practice and representative learning operationalizing much of what other schools call mental training. The mental side is built through training design.
- [[japanese-training]]: the Daimatsu-rooted tradition treats toughness and cohesion as outputs of training intensity itself, so high volume in competitive context is the culture-building mechanism.
- [[usa-volleyball]]: the Coach Academy curriculum lists Mind, Heart, and Team as three of its five pillars (alongside Craft and Body), treating mental-domain coach education as first-class.
- [[ecological-dynamics]]: declines to foreground "mental" as a separate construct, since perception-action coupling absorbs it. A reminder that mental and technical are less separable than older frames imply.
