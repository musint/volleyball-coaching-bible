Round one leaned on themes. This round is built from three research passes (platform guidance and WCAG numbers, best-in-class product teardowns, 2026 trend evidence). Every direction below is a working product language with named references, shown as the same Courtside screen with real wiki content. All four share the non-negotiables the research fixed: five labeled bottom tabs, 44px+ targets, 4.5:1 contrast, no glassmorphism, no bento, motion only for wayfinding.

The bet

Near-black OKLCH canvas, surface ladder + 1px hairlines (zero drop shadows), color is semantic only: green = on standard, amber = watch, red = risk. One oversized tabular numeral as the hero. Mono for numbers, grotesk for words.

Banks on

  • WHOOP's semantic-only palette and arm's-length hero numeral
  • Apple Sports density: variable width+weight numerals
  • Linear's hairline elevation keeping dense lists quiet

Verdict

  • + Best 3-second read in a dim gym; OLED battery; data feels like instrumentation
  • − Needs a real light theme for sunlit courts and printing; dark-first is a commitment, not a toggle

The bet

Ink on paper, engineered: #171717 on #fafafa, hairlines and stacked 1-2px shadows for depth, weight capped at 600, negative tracking on display only, exactly one accent (link blue) and it never decorates. Mono only for eyebrows and numerals. Search is the hero.

  • Contrast evidence: highest-leverage single decision under gym glare

  • − Zero atmosphere; needs perfect spacing/type execution or it reads as unstyled

The bet

One decision per screen. The M3 Expressive playbook applied literally: a single dominant action block in the deepest brand color with a contrasting yellow CTA, everything else in quiet white cards. Provenance stamped in mono on every row, Watch Duty style.

Banks on

  • The 18k-participant Expressive result: dominant element spotted up to 4x faster, older users match younger
